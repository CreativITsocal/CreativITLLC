04 About

From the classroom to the camera.

I spent sixteen years in the education realm as a teacher, Director of Technology, and Director of Digital Media. From the classroom to the school board, I learned how to not only spot the stories of impact happening on a daily basis, but also how to document and share them on every platform from social media to public billboards.

Today I bring the same vision, technical skill, and storytelling to organizations that want to be seen clearly and distinctly. I listen, plan, adapt, and tell your story.
