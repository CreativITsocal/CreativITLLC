04 About

Teacher by training. Storyteller by heart.

I’m Justin Royse, founder of CreativIT. I help organizations and individuals tell their story and share their purpose through video, photography, and digital media.

My story.

Sixteen years in a school taught me how to listen, explain, and earn trust. Those are the same skills that make a good story.

I began my career in education as a PE teacher and athletic director at a small private school in the Inland Empire. Over time I moved into the classroom and found my place teaching middle school math for more than ten years.

As the school committed to innovation and educational technology, I also took on the roles of Director of Technology and Director of Digital Media. That work ranged from overseeing the campus network and more than 200 computers and student devices to leading professional development for teachers.

The part I loved most, though, was telling the school’s story. I became its photographer, videographer, and social media manager, building a modern online presence that gave the community a real glimpse of who we were. I produced the school’s promotional videos and social graphics and designed billboard and magazine ad campaigns, putting my degrees in marketing and educational leadership to work.

Today, CreativIT is my full-time focus. I bring that same mix of creativity, technical skill, and care to every client, whether it’s a nonprofit gala, a team of first responders, or a family celebrating a graduate.
